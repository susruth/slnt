# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

SLNT is an experimental privacy and payments project. Work here can be
technical, adversarial, and high-stakes, but participation should still be
clear, respectful, and safe for contributors.

We pledge to make this project welcoming to people of different backgrounds,
experience levels, identities, and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas, designs, code, and threat models rather than people.
- Assume good intent until there is evidence otherwise.
- Make room for newcomers and for careful questions.
- Keep security and privacy reports confidential until disclosure is agreed.
- Accept maintainer decisions once a discussion has run its course.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, slurs, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, bad-faith arguments, or repeated off-topic comments.
- Publicly disclosing vulnerabilities before maintainers have had a fair
  chance to coordinate a fix.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces including issues, pull
requests, discussions, chats, events, and any public interaction where someone
is representing the SLNT project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict
participation, or ban contributors who violate this code of conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to <susruth@susruth.com>. If the report
involves a security or privacy vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, keep information as private as
practical, and choose a response proportionate to the situation.
