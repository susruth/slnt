THE WALLET GAP

Users should not choose between convenience and privacy.

Today, wallets have two awkward options: reuse one public address and expose a user's payment graph, or ask people to coordinate fresh addresses out of band. SLNT gives wallets a third path: a reusable meta-address that produces unlinkable one-time wallets for each payment.

WHAT WALLETS IMPLEMENT

A small standard for reusable, private receiving.

01

Meta-address

A shareable receiving address with spend and scan public material. Users can put it in a profile, QR code, checkout flow, or contact.

02

Stealth derivation

Senders derive a fresh Solana wallet for every payment. Recipients scan announcements and recognize only the outputs meant for them.

03

Discovery layer

Pinboard handles payment announcements. The optional registry maps wallet pubkeys to meta-addresses without custody or admin control.

STATUS

Open now, immutable after review.

SLNT is a draft standard for public review. Current devnet and testnet deployments remain upgradeable while v1 is draft and unaudited; the canonical v1 deployment is intended to become immutable as soon as v1 is finalized and independently audited.
